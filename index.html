A study of the tags

The quiet part of every website.

HTML names the parts of a page: the title, the paragraphs, the pictures, the links. These lessons practice each part, and they share one stylesheet so the set feels finished.

How these pages are built

  • One stylesheet. Every page links to css/styles.css. Color, type, and the header live there.
  • One h1. The big title is the h1. Sections under it use h2, then h3.
  • Open the source. The lesson is the markup. View source on any page and the structure is readable.
